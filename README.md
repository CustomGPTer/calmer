# Calmer

Calmer is a free anxiety self-help app for Android, built on cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT). It has quick check-ins, Calm now breathing and grounding, Thought Reframe, Worry Lab, Worry Time, Brave Steps, 37 lessons, ten five-day programmes, insights, a monthly GAD-7 questionnaire, a report for your GP or therapist, Health Connect, widgets and password-locked backups. It has no adverts, no accounts and no paid version, and it cannot go online.

Calmer is a self-help tool, not a crisis service. If you are in danger, call 999. Samaritans are free on 116 123.
