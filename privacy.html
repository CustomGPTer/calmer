Privacy policy

The short version

  • Everything you put into Calmer is kept on your phone, encrypted. It is never uploaded, to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Calmer does not hold Android's internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • Copies leave the app only when you make them: a backup, a report, a spreadsheet or a shared picture.
  • Health Connect is optional. Health data is read on your phone, shown to you and never stored in backups or exports.

What stays on your phone

Calmer keeps everything in one file in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The file is encrypted with AES-256 using a key held by Android's Keystore, so a copy taken off the phone cannot be read.

  • Your check-ins: the level, the time, and any feelings, body areas, triggers, what helped, sleep, caffeine and note you added.
  • Calm sessions: what you did, for how long, and your before and after ratings.
  • Thought records, worries and their outcomes, parked worries, Brave Steps ladders and their attempts.
  • Lessons read, programme steps done and the reflections you wrote.
  • Your GAD-7 answers and scores.
  • Your settings, including reminder times, the app's name and icon, and your app lock. The PIN itself is not stored: only a salted, one-way hash of it.
  • If you set a backup password: a key made from it, kept inside the encrypted file, so daily backups can be made without asking you each time.

Health Connect

If you turn Health Connect on, Calmer reads the types you allow, for the period you are looking at, and uses them on your phone to compare with your check-ins and to fill in last night's sleep. It does not keep a copy: the numbers are read again each time. They are not included in backups, spreadsheets or the monthly letters. They appear in a report only if you make one and choose to share it.

If you allow it, breathing and grounding sessions of a minute or more are saved to Health Connect as mindfulness sessions, with their title and times. You can remove the access at any time in Health Connect, and the data Calmer saved stays in Health Connect until you delete it there.

Notes and sensitive information

What you write about your anxiety is health information, and it is treated as such. It never leaves your phone unless you save or share a copy yourself.

When you type a note, Calmer checks it on your phone for words that suggest you might be in danger, so it can show a card with help lines. The check happens entirely on the phone. Nothing is sent, logged or remembered apart from the day the card was last shown, so it doesn't repeat.

Backups, reports and exports

These are made only when you ask, and go only where you choose:

  • Backup file. Everything except your PIN and the backup settings, compressed and encrypted with AES-256 using a key made from your backup password. Without the password it cannot be opened, by anyone.
  • Daily backup. The same file, saved to the one folder you picked, once a day when something has changed. The newest seven are kept and older ones deleted. If that folder is synced to a cloud service, that service's own privacy terms apply to the copy.
  • Report. A PDF of the period you choose. Notes are left out unless you switch them on.
  • Spreadsheets. CSV files of your check-ins and other records. These are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.
  • Share picture. A monthly or yearly picture showing numbers and colours only, never your notes.

When you share a file, Android's share sheet passes it to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy terms apply.

Widgets and notifications

The sky widget shows the colours of this week's check-ins and the name you gave the app. The check-in and Calm now widgets show no data at all. Notifications are marked private, so your lock screen shows only the app's name, and their wording never mentions anxiety.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete any single check-in, session, thought, worry or questionnaire result, or everything at once in Settings, under Your data. Uninstalling Calmer deletes everything the app holds. Backup files, reports and spreadsheets you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands: you can see it in the app, export it in full and delete it at any time.

Security

The data file is encrypted with a key that never leaves Android's Keystore. The app lock uses a PIN of four to six digits, stored only as a PBKDF2 hash, and pauses after five wrong tries. Android's own app backup is switched off for Calmer, so your entries are not copied to a Google account without your knowledge. No method is perfect: keep your phone locked and your backup password private.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. Downloads come from GitHub's servers on the same terms.

Children

Calmer is made for adults. It collects nothing from anyone, but its lessons and tools are written for adults, and young people are best supported by a trusted adult, their GP or a service made for their age.
