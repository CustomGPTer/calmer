Lessons, sounds and voice

The lessons, programmes and prompts were written for Calmer by the developer, drawing on widely taught CBT and ACT methods, in his own words. The rain, sea and soft-noise sounds are generated by the app as it plays, so no recordings are used. Spoken cues use the text-to-speech voice already on your phone.
