What Calmer does not do

Calmer is self-help, not treatment, and it has no therapists, coaches or community. It has no guided meditations or sleep stories. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and install new versions over the old one.

Where the others do more

Sanvello offers therapy and coaching in the United States. Calm has a large library of meditations, music and sleep stories. MindShift CBT is on Google Play and updates itself.

If you are in crisis

No app is a substitute for help. In the UK call 999 in an emergency, or Samaritans free on 116 123, any time.

If something here is out of date

Apps change. If a row is no longer right, open an issue on GitHub and it will be corrected.
