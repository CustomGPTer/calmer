What the others do well

AnxietyPulse has a very quick check-in, achievements and Siri shortcuts, and it is available in twelve languages. Sanvello offers coaching and therapy with real people in the United States, and a community. Calm has a large library of guided meditations and sleep stories. Calmer does none of those things: it is a private, self-guided toolkit in English.
