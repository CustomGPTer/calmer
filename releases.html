LatestReleased 21 November 2026

Version 1.0: first release

Added

  • Check-ins: a 0 to 10 slider with plain words for each level, feelings, a body map, triggers, what helped, sleep, caffeine and a note. Earlier times and days can be chosen, and any check-in can be edited or deleted with undo.
  • Home: a living sky that follows the time of day and your week, a week of coloured dots, and cards for whatever needs you next.
  • Calm now: breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, a ride-the-wave timer for panic and the help lines, in one place.
  • Six breathing patterns and your own, with orb, tide and box visuals, vibration pacing, spoken cues, generated rain, sea and soft noise, and before-and-after ratings.
  • Thought Reframe, with eleven thinking traps and before-and-after belief ratings.
  • Worry Lab, Worry Time with a private daily reminder, and Brave Steps with four ready-made ladders.
  • Learn: 37 short lessons in eight topics, ten five-day programmes and a list of your reflections.
  • Insights: time of day and weekday maps, trigger links, the tools that help you most, sleep and caffeine, skills practised, each pattern marked as an early sign, an emerging pattern or a clear pattern.
  • A monthly GAD-7 questionnaire, optional, with your scores over time.
  • A PDF report for your GP or therapist, with a preview, and a choice of period, notes and detail.
  • Monthly and yearly letters, and a picture to share that shows numbers only.
  • Health Connect: sleep, steps, resting heart rate and heart rate variability in Insights and the report, last night's sleep in a new check-in, and calm sessions saved as mindfulness.
  • Widgets: one-tap check-in, this week's sky, and Calm now, which opens even with the app lock on.
  • Reminders for a check-in and for today's programme step, as well as Worry Time: never more than two a day, never in quiet hours, worded so they stay private.
  • Backup: a password-locked backup file, a daily copy to a folder you choose keeping the newest seven, and restore with undo. Spreadsheets of every part of your data.
  • A gentle support card after a sustained heavy stretch, and a crisis card when a note suggests you may be in danger.
  • App lock with a PIN or fingerprint, and a choice of home-screen name and eight pastel icons.
  • Light and dark themes, five accent colours, and a reduce-motion setting.
